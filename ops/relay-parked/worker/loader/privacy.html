What the relay can and can't see

It can't read or change your traffic. Your devices and your Josi server encrypt everything end to end. The relay only passes sealed messages along, and it has no key to open them.

It can see: your address, that your server is online, when devices connect, their internet addresses, and roughly how much data moves.

One limit in a browser: this small starter page comes from the relay, so a browser has to trust it. The apps don't have this limit. On a shared or sensitive setup, use an app, your own network, or your own relay.

Signing in: your Hey Josi account can't open your server. You still sign in to Josi itself.
