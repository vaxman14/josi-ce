Privacy

What Hey Josi holds, and how to leave.

What we can and can’t see

We can see: your email, your address, your plan and payments, the names of your paired devices, and how much AI you use, as token counts and cost.

We can’t see: what passes between your devices and your server. It’s encrypted end to end, and the relay only moves sealed bytes. This account can’t sign in to your server.

Passing through: AI requests go through our gateway to OpenAI. We count their cost and don’t keep their text. Google sign-in tokens go through us to your server, and we don’t keep those either.

Delete your account

This cancels your plan, gives up your address and removes your data here. Your Josi server keeps working on your own network. It can’t be undone.
