# Josi Browser Control: privacy policy

**Effective date: 9 November 2026.** Publisher: SOCAL RECEPTIONIST LLC.
Published at https://heyjosi.com/browser-extension/privacy (the same text).

Josi Browser Control is a browser extension that lets Josi, the assistant app
installed on your own computer, use your web browser when you ask it to. It
works only together with that app. It has no servers of its own, shows no ads,
and does not sell or share data with anyone.

## What the extension reads, and when

- **When you ask Josi to do something in your browser**, and only within the
  permissions you set in Josi's PC Control (or approve in Josi's chat), the
  extension reads the page Josi is using: its address, title, visible text,
  the names of buttons, links and fields, and, one at a time, the pictures
  Josi is asked to look at. It can type into a field, choose an option,
  click, scroll, drag, download a file from that site, or put a file you named
  in Josi into an upload field. Each of these is checked by the Josi app before
  it happens.
- **While the browser is linked to Josi**, the extension tells the Josi app the
  site and title of your open tabs, so you can name a tab in chat ("go to my
  Gmail tab"). Not their content, and never private (incognito) windows.
- **Never**: passwords, payment card fields, one-time codes or ID numbers. The
  extension skips those fields entirely; it neither reads nor fills them. It
  does not read your browsing history, cookies or saved passwords, and it does
  not run code downloaded from anywhere.

## Where it goes

Everything the extension reads goes over your browser's native messaging
channel to the Josi app on the same computer, and nowhere else directly. The
Josi app then handles it like the rest of your conversation with Josi: it is
sent to your Josi server (which you or your organization runs) and to the AI
model provider you chose in Josi, so the assistant can answer you. Those are
governed by your Josi installation and that provider's terms; see the Josi
privacy notice at https://heyjosi.com/privacy.

## What it keeps

In your browser: a random identifier for this browser profile, and, until the
browser closes, which tab Josi is using. Files Josi downloads go to your
browser's Downloads folder. Nothing else is stored by the extension.

## Your control

Josi asks before each new site and action unless you allowed it in PC Control.
"Take control" in the extension's popup stops Josi in this browser at once;
Pause and Stop in the Josi app stop all of its work. Removing the extension
removes everything it stored.
