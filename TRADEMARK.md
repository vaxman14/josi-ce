## Why this file is separate from the LICENSE

Josi CE's **code** is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License
version 3 (see `LICENSE`). That license governs copying, modifying and
distributing software.

It does **not** govern trademarks. Copyright and trademark are different bodies
of law, and the AGPL says so itself — section 7 permits additional terms
declining to grant rights under trademark law. The Josi name, the shepherd
logo, the wordmark and the overall product identity are **not** covered by the
AGPL grant and are addressed here instead.

In plain terms: you may take the code, change it, and run or redistribute it
under the AGPL. You may not use it to present your version as the official Josi
product.

## Branding is required in Josi CE and may not be replaced

Josi CE ships with official Josi branding, and self-hosters may not remove or
substitute it. This is a deliberate condition of the distribution: it is how a
user can tell that the thing in front of them is Josi.

Specifically, you may **not**:

- remove the Josi name or mark from the running application in order to present
  the same software under a different product identity;
- replace the Josi branding with your own or a client's branding
  (this is white-labelling, and it is a separate commercial arrangement — see
  below);
- imply that a modified version is the official Josi product, or that it is
  endorsed, supported, certified or approved by SOCAL RECEPTIONIST LLC;
- use the Josi name or mark as, or as part of, the name of your fork, product,
  company, domain or app-store listing.

**[REVIEW]** — the interaction between a mandatory-branding condition and AGPL
§7 needs a lawyer's eye. AGPL §7(e) permits terms "declining to grant rights
under trademark law", and preserving legal notices is permitted under §7(b), but
a requirement that goes further than trademark law allows could be argued to be
a further restriction under §7 and therefore removable by a downstream
recipient. Counsel should determine how much of the requirement above is
enforceable as a trademark condition versus how much needs to be reframed.

## What you may do without asking

- Run Josi CE, modified or unmodified, for yourself, your business or your
  clients' installations.
- State truthfully that your work is **"based on Josi CE"**, **"a fork of Josi
  CE"**, or **"compatible with Josi"**, provided it is clear you are not the
  origin of Josi and not endorsed by SOCAL RECEPTIONIST LLC.
- Use the Josi name in ordinary descriptive and referential ways: documentation,
  tutorials, blog posts, comparisons, bug reports, conference talks.
- Redistribute Josi CE **unmodified**, with its branding intact.

## What requires a separate written agreement

- **White-labelling** — shipping Josi under your own or a client's brand.
- **Multi-workspace or multi-tenant operation.** Josi CE is one workspace per
  installation by design. Operating Josi as a multi-tenant service for third
  parties requires a licence.
- Any use of the Josi name or mark in your own product name, company name or
  marketing that implies origin or endorsement.

Contact SOCAL RECEPTIONIST LLC to discuss white-label licensing or the hosted
service.
